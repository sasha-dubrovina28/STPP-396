About the Game

A puzzle journey with heart and community Jolly Match 3 invites players into a cozy world where a tornado has scattered cookies, gems, and candies across once-beautiful locations. By solving match-3 levels, players earn resources to restore these places while unlocking helper characters, joining teams, and taking part in seasonal events.

How to Play

Join a Team

Team up with players worldwide for shared events and rewards.

Restore Storm-Damaged Locations

Use earned resources to rebuild each area. Choose a Helper Character
